## Reference forensics (v4)
- The Short sits at y 181–1101 of the recording (512×920). The recording catches it looping. The loop point is the
  cut at 18.10 s (NOV card → BLACK FRIDAY title). The first 0.9 s of the recording repeat 3.5–4.4 s, so the
  playback was resumed there. **Film time = recording time − 0.13 s**, and the Short is **18.0 s** (540 frames
  at 30 fps).
- Music (onsets measured, cuts land on kicks within 1–3 frames):
  - quiet intro with a riser until the **drop at 2.90 s**, on the cut to the red-lit wheel;
  - a hard **stop at 14.90 s**, on the cut to the final shot;
  - room tone only until the loop.
- Typography:
  - **BLACK FRIDAY title card:** a HUD frame with corner crosses and circled-x markers, a red label with the
    wordmark, a techno display face, the date in small caps on the bottom rule, WORLDWIDE SALE below, and the
    brand name set vertically top-left.
  - **Offer lines:** heavy condensed italic (SAVE UP TO in red, $700 white, PER SET small).
  - **Dates:** a red square with NOV 24 / THRU (outline) / DEC 1. The letters type on and off, and the walker
    passes in front of the square (roto).
  - **Small print:** © line at the bottom. The brand wordmark appears on the last shot.
