# Turismo transformation reel (rebuild of the VANTA tuning reel)

**Reference:** a VANTA Tuning Instagram reel, Sept 2026, received as two WhatsApp copies:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `source/ref_vanta_clean_512x850.mp4` | Clean picture, 18.03 s. **Use this one.** |
| `source/ref_vanta_instagram_capture.mp4` | 512×1328 phone screen recording with the Instagram UI (account vanta.tuning, caption "VANTA \| Next Level Tuning Shop"), 17.99 s. The UI is not part of the film. |

**Format:** vertical (the clean copy is about 9:15, a crop of 9:16), about 66 fps variable on the capture.
Audio: a music track at 120 BPM, −14.2 LUFS, wall to wall.

**What it is:** full CGI. A car-tuning transformation shot as FPV-drone moves. A yellow Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992)
drives on a coastal road with palms; a light-blue aftermarket hood flies in and mounts itself; the camera dives
through the hood vent and comes out in a snowy mountain world where the whole car is now light blue with an active
rear wing. It ends on the wheels. A bare wheel and tyre spinning on black open and close the reel, so it loops.
**There is no on-screen text.**

## Rebuild plan (Turismo)

The reference is someone else's CGI, so the rebuild generates new footage: no VANTA frames are repainted.

1. **Keyframes:** still images, one per shot, of the car with **Turismo wheels** (from the client's product photos)
   in both worlds. These lock the car, colours and wheels across shots.
2. **Shots:** image-to-video in Seedance 2.5 from each keyframe (start, and end frame where shots connect), with
   the reference's camera moves described per shot, at 9:16.
3. **Assembly:** cut on the reference's beat points to 18.0 s, as a 1080×1920 loop.
4. **Score:** a licence-free 120 BPM track, with drops and the breakdown on the same marks.
5. **QC:** as in the promo-remake skill.

**Open decisions for the client:**
- the wheel model and photos,
- whether to keep the car or use another,
- what transforms: keep the hood (as VANTA) or, as recommended for a wheel brand, transform the wheels themselves,
- whether to add a Turismo logo on the black end frame (the reference has none),
- budget.
