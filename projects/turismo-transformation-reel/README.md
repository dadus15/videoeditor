**Format:** vertical (the clean copy is about 9:15, a crop of 9:16), about 66 fps variable on the capture.

**What it is:** full CGI. A car-tuning transformation shot as FPV-drone moves. A yellow Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992)

through the hood vent and comes out in a snowy mountain world where the whole car is now light blue with an active
rear wing. It ends on the wheels. A bare wheel and tyre spinning on black open and close the reel, so it loops.
**There is no on-screen text.**

## v2: pro finishing (3 Nov 2026), after "too artificial" feedback

**Diagnosis of v1:**
- The 24 fps AI clips were assembled at 25 fps by dropping or duplicating frames: 5 repeated frames in the
  transformation, and very uneven motion in the chase (irregularity 0.84).
- The white flash was a cheap transition.
- Every clip kept its own over-saturated "AI" colour.

**Fixes, with no extra credits** (`.claude/skills/promo-remake/scripts/reel_finish.py`, plan `finish_plan_v2.json`):
- All clips are optical-flow interpolated to 96 fps (ffmpeg minterpolate, 4 parallel jobs on an XL sandbox, about
  4 min).
- Output is 30 fps with eased speed ramps (out, in, inout) sampled from the 96 fps frames, so nothing is dropped or
  repeated.
- Motion blur scales with speed (180° shutter).
- Real transitions on the beats:
  - impact at 1.0 s,
  - whip at 3.0 s,
  - zoom at 7.7 s,
  - spin-zoom through the cap at 8.9 s, instead of the white flash,
  - whip at 10.3 s,
  - impacts on the hard cuts.
- Drone micro-shake on the FPV shots.
- One filmic grade: hot oranges desaturated, S-curve, lifted blacks, warm halation, grain, vignette, 1 px lens CA.

**QC:**

- 0 repeated frames in moving shots;

## v5: honest reference vs v4 comparison, and the free fixes (3 Nov 2026)

The client's verdict on v4 was that it still lacks the reference's "WOW"; the animation, motion and overall feel
differ a lot. A 4 fps side-by-side of the full 18 s (`analysis/`, same layout for both) confirms it.

**Gaps, most important first:**
1. **Look.** The reference's first world is a bright overcast seaside: white sky, neutral to cool, low
   contrast, a clean render. Ours is an orange golden-hour sunset with HDR drama, so the mood of the first half
   differs. A grade cannot fix it; it needs regeneration.
2. **Removal beat (2.25–2.5 s).** The old hood violently pops off and flies away. We have no equivalent: the
   stock wheels just stay on.
3. **Hero presentation.** One new part floats large and centred for 4 s, turning slowly, and lands while the
   camera travels with the car. Ours drops two wheels diagonally from the side, in a busy frame.
4. **Dive through the paint.** The camera dives into the hood vent, the frame becomes flat paint, and it exits
   through the side window into the snow world with the car in a new colour (yellow → light blue). This is the
   main WOW. Ours dives into the cap, but the car is white before and after.
5. **Rear wing (10.5–11 s).** The active wing rises as the car pulls away. We have no equivalent.
6. **Motion.** The reference has choreographed CGI camera paths. Ours comes from start/end keyframe clips, so
   the AI invents the in-between motion and it floats.

**Root cause:** this reel skipped the skill's core method, a Blender motion guide repainted with Seedance
`video_edit`, which made the C19R film work. A full v5 on that method would cost about 200 credits; 14 are left.
The client chose free fixes only, and a colour change: another colour before, white after.

**Free fixes done:**
- **Colour change, as in the reference.** `recolor_car.py` repaints the car yellow in every shot before the dive:
  - FPV `vFy` (`--guard 0 --sat-max 0.6`),
  - transformation `vTy` (`--sat-max 0.45`),
  - dive `v4y` (`--to 84`, then fading back before the cap).

  The car comes out of the cap macro white on gold MSP02 in the snow, so it reads yellow → white with new wheels.

  Method: CLIPSeg "a car" ∪ u2net as the car mask, CLIPSeg "a wheel rim" dilated as the guard for chrome and gold,
  then paint (bright, low saturation) retinted with its shading kept.
- **A cleaner, render-like grade:** grain 0.005, halation 0.03, vignette 0.12, hot_sat 0.86 so the yellow stays
  rich. Plan `finish_plan_v5.json`.

**Still open (needs credits):** gaps 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 above.

### Pipeline

The method is "Playblast2Render", the industry approach found in research:
1. A grey-box 3D animation with the reference's exact choreography.
2. Seedance `video_edit` repaints it photoreal, with the client's photos as references.
3. Motion and timing stay locked to the playblast.

- **Car model:** Hunyuan3D v3 Geometry from the three studio photos (11 credits). It came out at 497k triangles
  and is a recognisable GT3 RS with swan-neck wing, bonnet vents and mesh wheels.
  - Job: `4cdddcc3-06db-4ecc-a566-353e4262ade5`.
  - Scale ×4.24 gives 4.57 m long and a 2.46 m wheelbase, exactly as the real car.
  - `cgi/guide.py` splits it by geometry into body, bonnet, wing and four wheels.
- **Rendering**, about 1,000× cheaper in time than the C19R Cycles guide:
  - Blender 4.5 in the Higgsfield sandbox, BLENDER_WORKBENCH (EEVEE needs a GPU; on CPU it is 4–50 s a frame);
  - 360×640 at 24 fps, 294 frames, 6 parallel processes on the XL sandbox, about 10 min.
  - Local tests on the same frame:

    | Engine | Time per frame |
    |---|---|
    | Workbench | 0.64 s |
    | EEVEE on llvmpipe | 3.9 s |
    | Cycles, 4 spp | 5.7 s |
    | C19R Cycles | ~20 s |
- **Prompts:** `seedance_prompts_v6.md`, written with the cinema-worldbuilder skill.
- **Cost of the final pass:** `video_edit` is billed by source length, about 7.6 credits/s at 720p. Segments A
  (7.7 s) and B (4.6 s) come to about 94 credits, plus patches. Balance: 3 credits after the model.
