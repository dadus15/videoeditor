# DESIGN 01: blade split-spoke (approved October 2026)

The client's verdict was "perfekcyjnie". The render was made with the crop-and-trace method from `DESIGN02.md`.

## Spec

- **Spokes:** 5 split spokes. Each leaves the hub as one spoke and splits into a V of two arms, so 10 arms reach the rim. The pairs are asymmetric and slightly twisted, which gives a directional swirl.
- **Arms:** sharply faceted with a centre ridge, concave toward the barrel, in brushed light-gunmetal aluminium with diamond-cut edges.
- **Purple:**
  - candy-violet teardrop or lancet inlays between the arms of each pair, near the hub;
  - a thin candy-violet ring at the boundary between face and lip.
- **Lip and barrel:** a very wide, flat, stepped, mirror-polished lip with no bolts. The barrel is gloss black.
- **Hub:** 5 gloss black lug bolts, with 5 small screw dots between them.
- **Center cap:** a thick candy-violet ring around a polished silver cap. The logo is in its original colours.
- **No center cover cap.**
