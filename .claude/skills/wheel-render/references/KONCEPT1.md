# KONCEPT1: Audi RS6 C7 one-off (in progress, October 2026)

## Brief

- Second sheet from the same artist, for an Audi RS6 C7, rendered with this skill.

## Sketch reading

- **Spokes:** 10 straight spokes, each split lengthwise by a long milled slot, so each one reads as a slim twin spoke. The face is moderately concave.
- **Aero ring:** a ring of U-shaped vent pockets around the outer edge of the face.
- **Lip:** a very deep, stepped deep-dish lip, with a purple pinstripe on its edge.
- **Hub:** 5 purple lug nuts, with small rectangular tabs between the lug seats.

## Spec as rendered

- Spokes in satin graphite brushed aluminium with diamond-cut edges, and candy-violet inside every slot.
- U-shaped aero vents in gloss black with purple inner walls.
- A mirror-polished, seamless lip with a purple pinstripe. 2PC with hidden hardware.
- A gloss black barrel. No carbon.
- **Hub: NO center cover cap.** The client dropped it after the first set. Instead: 5 visible candy-violet lug nuts and a small gloss black center cap with the chrome logo.

## Lessons

- Don't carry the cover cap over from an earlier project by default. Follow the sketch's hub, or ask.
- Using KONCEPT2 renders as references risks copying KONCEPT2's spokes into the new design. Limit each reference to one part ("use the third image ONLY for the cap / scene") and check the spokes against the sketch.
