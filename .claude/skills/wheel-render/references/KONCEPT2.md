# KONCEPT2: Audi RS6 C7 one-off (approved October 2026)

## Brief

- One-off wheel for an Audi RS6 Avant C7, based on the artist's pencil sheet "KONCEPT2".
- Required: purple accents, a center cover cap with the Turismo Wheels logo, an aero ring, a polished lip, and a "WOW" effect.

## Final spec

- 21" forged design with 10 slim, faceted, concave spokes in satin dark graphite brushed aluminium, with diamond-cut edges.
- A candy-violet inlay channel in every spoke, and purple-framed pockets near the rim.
- A deep mirror-polished lip. 2PC with hidden hardware, so the face shows no bolts.
- A gloss black barrel and inner area. No carbon.
- An aero ring at the outer perimeter, in black with a purple pinstripe.
- Hub: the Titanium center cover cap (three levels, see `WHEEL-ANATOMY.md`). On car scenes where the client approved it, a small gloss black center cap with a chrome logo.
- Car: Nardo grey RS6 C7 with purple calipers, in a night industrial hall with purple and white neons on wet asphalt.

## Lessons from this project

- Text-only first passes looked good, but the logo was only approximate. Upload the logo before the first serious generation.
- The client called the carbon aero ring seen through the spokes "the barrel". Treat any texture behind the spokes as part of the barrel.
- "Center cover cap" means the full hub cover. A small cap or a cap study board is not it. Ask for, or analyse, a reference when the term comes up.
- One edit that was sent without the logo reference changed the logo. Attach the logo and the logo master to every edit.
