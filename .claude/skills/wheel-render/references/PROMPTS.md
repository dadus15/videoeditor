# Prompt templates that worked

Put the references in the order the prompt names them. Write prompts in English.

## Logo clause (paste into every prompt)

> The center cap shows the logo from the [N-th] reference image EXACTLY: a forward-leaning italic letter T built from two slanted parallelogram blocks, one small red square in the lower left corner, the word TURISMO beneath in wide geometric sans-serif capitals, same proportions, nothing added or changed. [Material: raised chrome on gloss black | engraved and filled gloss black on brushed titanium]; the red square stays red.

## From sketch to first render

References: the sketch, then the logo.

> Turn the hand-drawn wheel sketch from the first reference image into a hyper-photorealistic, refined, production-ready studio render of a single one-off custom forged 21-inch aluminium wheel for [CAR]. Keep the sketch's spoke layout faithfully: [spec]. Refine it like a professional wheel designer: [finish details]. [Aero ring / lip / barrel / cap clauses]. [Logo clause]. Composition: three-quarter view, slightly tilted, floating above a glossy black reflective floor. Lighting: dark moody studio, big overhead softbox, crisp white strip reflections on the polished lip, violet rim light from behind, subtle haze and volumetric light, cinematic contrast, Keyshot quality. Small caption lower right: "TURISMO [MODEL] | one of one". No other text, no watermark, no pencil lines.

## Frontal wheel face (4:5, the shot the client trusted most for the logo)

> Hyper-photorealistic straight-on frontal render of the full wheel face … black studio background, symmetrical composition, violet rim glow, crisp softbox reflections, floating dust particles, luxury catalogue photography.

## 3/4 design card (1:1)

This is styled after the client's VIOLET/001 card.

> Three-quarter view showing the wheel face and the deep barrel behind it, standing on a dark glossy floor with purple reflections … Typography overlay top left in clean thin spaced sans-serif: large purple text '[MODEL] / 001' and below in small grey caps '[SUBTITLE]', with a short purple line under it.

## Exploded view of the center cover cap (16:9)

Use this only when the project has a cover cap. KONCEPT1 dropped it.

> Technical EXPLODED VIEW product render … components floating apart along one axis in front of the hub … From back to front: hub with 5 lug bolts on a 5x112 bolt circle and a black anodized base retaining ring with hex screws; OUTER BEZEL …; castellated MIDDLE LOCK RING …; INNER LOGO DOME … Thin white leader lines with small labels: 'BASE RING', 'BEZEL', 'LOCK RING', 'LOGO CAP'.

## Car scene (16:9)

> Hyper-photorealistic automotive commercial photo: low-angle front three-quarter shot of a [colour] [car], lowered, on wet dark asphalt in an empty industrial hall at night … front wheel as the hero in sharp focus … violet and cool white neon strip reflections on the paint and wet floor, light haze, 35mm lens, magazine cover quality. No text, no watermark.

## Edit template (use for every revision)

References: the image being edited, then the logo file, then an approved render with a correct logo.

> Edit the FIRST reference image. Keep EVERYTHING identical: [car/scene/camera/lighting/spokes/purple inlays/lip/cap/logo …]. CHANGE ONLY [ONE/TWO] THING[S]: [precise change, with "no X, no Y" exclusions]. The logo on the center cap must stay exactly as in the SECOND and THIRD reference images ([logo clause]). Everything else stays exactly the same. No text, no watermark.

Examples the client approved:
- **Black barrel:** "the barrel and the whole inner area visible behind and between the spokes … solid, uniform deep GLOSS BLACK finish, no silver, no grey, no carbon texture, so the purple accents pop".
- **Hidden hardware:** "remove ALL visible assembly bolts, rivets and screw heads from the wheel face and from around the polished lip. This is a 2-piece wheel with hidden hardware (assembly bolts mounted from the inner/back side of the barrel), so the front shows a perfectly clean, seamless mirror-polished lip".
- **Removing the cover cap (KONCEPT1):** "remove the large titanium center cover cap completely (no TURISMO/WHEELS bezel, no gear-toothed ring). Show an open hub with 5 visible lug nuts in glossy candy-violet purple and a small gloss black center cap like the one in the THIRD reference image". The third reference is the logo master `1ec659bd…`, which has a small cap.
